# ⚽ FC Dominators Football Club Website

A modern, responsive website for **FC Dominators**, designed to showcase the club, its teams, achievements, fixtures, gallery, and community involvement. The website serves as the club's online presence, making it easy for players, parents, supporters, and sponsors to stay connected.

![FC Dominators Banner](https://via.placeholder.com/1200x500?text=FC+Dominators)

---

## 📖 About

FC Dominators is a community football club dedicated to developing young talent, promoting teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship, while creating opportunities for players to grow both on and off the field.

This website was built to provide:
- Club information
- Team profiles
- Match fixtures and results
- News and announcements
- Player testimonials
- Parent testimonials
- Photo gallery
- Contact information
- Online registration (optional)
